## What the runs show

- **The same pattern as credit, and larger.** With Haiku, `csv` scores 44%, and the definitions take it to 96–97% (+52 to +54 points). RDF alone and the length control add nothing, and Markdown matches Turtle.
- **The local rules carry part of the gain, not all of it.** They go from 14–20% to 99–100%. The general questions go from 56–58% to 96–98%, because many rest on the dataset's own terms, which only the ontology defines.

A Sonnet run, and `csv` against `executed` for both models, are under way.
