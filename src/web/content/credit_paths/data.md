The 300-issuer data, rebuilt on the current rules, plus six invented groups four to six levels deep, written by `paths.py`. The groups' entities are `E5001` onwards, and `anchors.json` names the designed ones: each group's top and deepest member, the stale parent claim, the member in Valdoria, the cross-group guarantee and the QA record inside a group.

The `executed` tables add `ancestors.csv`: every entity above each entity in its chain of parents, with how many levels up.
