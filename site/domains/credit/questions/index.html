All data is invented: the companies, LEIs, ratings, works, people and institutions. Nothing here is any rating agency's or bibliographic database's data or definitions.

Credit ratings: questions and traps

Each question looks like a lookup, and each has an obvious wrong answer. Only the question text is sent to the model, which must give its answer as a typed JSON field. The trap and the key are never sent. Keys are computed from the data, and each base key is checked against one written by hand, so the two can't silently disagree. The keys shown are the base data's; the other datasets compute their own.

The implicit meanings

Implicit meaning What it says How the data sets it Questions
When A rating holds from one action to the next, so "the rating on a date" is the latest action on or before it Kestrel Energy (E01) was A- from March 2024 and BBB from June 2025 (Q1). Marlow Utilities' (E14) actions are out of date order in the file (Q11). Halden Foods (E12) had no rating before its first action in February 2024 (Q14) Q1, Q11 to Q16
Outlook and Watch An outlook or a Rating Watch says where a rating may go. Neither is a rating change Kestrel was affirmed at BBB with a Negative outlook in 2026, not downgraded (Q2). Orrin Bank (E06) was placed on Watch Negative in September 2026 at an unchanged A- (Q17). Marlow's Watch ended with its May 2026 affirmation (Q16, Q18) Q2, Q16 to Q19
Withdrawn isn't defaulted WD is a withdrawal, often for commercial reasons; RD and D are defaults Pellam Retail's (E07) rating was withdrawn "for commercial reasons" in May 2026 (Q20, Q21). Saxby Shipping (E08) went to RD after a distressed debt exchange (Q8, Q22) Q8, Q20 to Q22
What is rated Issuer, instrument, short-term, local-currency: each is a different rating Kestrel is BBB, its subordinated 2031 note BB+ (Q3, Q24) and its short-term rating F2 (Q23). Corvenia Power (E15) is A in foreign currency and A+ in local (Q26). Valdoria Telecom (E05) has no single rating (Q10) Q3, Q10, Q23 to Q27
Ceilings A Country Ceiling caps foreign-currency ratings in a country. It isn't the sovereign's rating Valdoria's ceiling is BB, and the Republic has no issuer rating (Q29). Valdoria Telecom is BB in foreign currency, at the ceiling, and BBB- in local (Q6). Corvenia's ceiling is A, its own rating BBB+ (Q28) Q6, Q28 to Q31
Groups and support Ownership comes from parent links, not names; guaranteed debt carries the guarantor's risk Halden Leasing (E11) sits under Halden Bank (E10), under Halden Group (E09) (Q32). Its note is guaranteed by Halden Bank, not the group (Q33). Halden Foods (E12) shares the name but not the group (Q34). Northwind Finance's (E04) note is guaranteed by Northwind Holdings (Q5) Q5, Q32 to Q36
Who is who Same LEI, same entity; similar names, different entities Northwind Holdings has two records with one LEI, E02 and E03, and its latest action is on the second (Q4, Q39). Orrin Bank AG (E06) and Orrin Bancorp Inc (E13) have different LEIs (Q38) Q4, Q37 to Q40

The local meanings

Each rule is stated only in the ontology, and none can be guessed from a code's name or from general conventions.

Local rule How the data sets it Where the ontology states it Questions
QA records are test data: they and their actions are ignored Brindle Capital (E16) comes from the QA system, with rating actions that look real: an assignment and a 2026 downgrade lad:sourceSystem Q41 (also Q9, Q37, Q40)
Where records disagree, RESEARCH holds for country, RATINGS for name and parent Halden Bank's RATINGS record (E10) puts it in Germany, its RESEARCH record (E17) in Luxembourg lad:sourceSystem; lad:country Q42, Q43
XVD is the Valdorian dollar, CVK the Corvenian krone: local-currency debt, not capped Valdoria Telecom's 2027 note (I07) pays in XVD and is rated BBB-, above Valdoria's BB ceiling, which caps only foreign-currency ratings lad:currency Q44, Q45
CO is a credit opinion, which isn't a rating Halden Leasing's only issuer-level action is a CO at BBB lad:type-CO Q46
EX is an expected rating, which isn't a rating until it's assigned Corvenia Power's 2034 note (I08) has only an expected A lad:action-EX Q47, Q48
Q1What was Kestrel Energy's rating on 1 January 2025?

The trap: Time. A rating holds from one action to the next, so 'the rating on a date' is the latest action on or before it.

Expected: rating A- · graded by code

Q2Has Kestrel Energy been downgraded in 2026?

The trap: Outlook versus rating. An affirmation with a negative outlook is not a rating change.

Expected: downgraded no · graded by code

The trap: Issuer versus instrument rating. A subordinated instrument is notched below the issuer.

The trap: Identity. Two records with the same LEI are one entity, whatever their names.

Q6Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Graded against a checklist:

  • Says the ceiling caps foreign-currency ratings and not local-currency ones
