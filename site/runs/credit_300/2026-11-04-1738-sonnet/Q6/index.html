Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The foreign-currency rating is held to Valdoria's Country Ceiling of BB (A12). E05's FC rating is BB (A14) against an LC rating of BBB- (A13).

Why: Names the BB Country Ceiling as the cause, but never says the ceiling caps foreign-currency ratings and not local-currency ones; the LC vs FC ratings are only listed.

The Country Ceiling caps it. VD's ceiling is BB (A12), and E05's foreign-currency rating is BB (A14) while its local-currency rating is BBB- (A13).

Why: Names the Country Ceiling of BB as the cause, but never says it caps foreign-currency ratings and not local-currency ones; it only implies this through the example ratings.
