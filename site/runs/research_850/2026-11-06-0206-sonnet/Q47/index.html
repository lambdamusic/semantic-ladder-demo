Why do Kesting University and Rostval Technical University swap places in 2024 between full and fractional counting?

Tests: Counting method: why the ranking flips.

Expected: Rostval is on 16 works in 2024, but on every one of them it has at most a quarter of the authors, so its shares add up to 4. Kesting is on 13, 8 of them by Kesting authors alone, so its shares add up to 11.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

Rostval has more works in 2024 under full counting (16 against 15). Each of its works has four authors with only one at Rostval, so it earns 0.25 per work, or 4.0 in total. Kesting's works are mostly written by its own authors, so it earns 13.0 and leads under fractional counting.

Why: Explains Rostval's small share (four authors, one from Rostval) but only vaguely says Kesting's works are mostly by its own authors, without clear larger-share reasoning such as few co-authors; the Rostval point is met, Kesting point is roughly met but thin.
