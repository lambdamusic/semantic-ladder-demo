Why is W70 not one of the University of Albury's research works, though its author lists the Tessel Laboratory?

Tests: Local rules: the last-listed affiliation, and affiliated institutes.

Expected: Tomasz Wierzba lists the Tessel Laboratory first and the Halloran Institute second; the house rule credits the last-listed one, Halloran. Halloran is an affiliated institute (AFF): its parent link is an agreement, so its works are not the University of Albury's.

❌ R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The premise is wrong. W70 is credited to I04 (a unit of I03 and I02) and to I06 (a child of I02), so it is one of the Albury units' research works and is counted in Q9.

Why: Rejects the premise, while the key says W70 is credited only to the last-listed affiliation (Halloran Institute), which is an affiliated institute that does not roll up to Albury.

❌ R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

The premise is false: W70 is counted in the University of Albury's output. **Reasoning:** The Tessel Laboratory (I04) sits under I03, which sits under I02 (University of Albury), and W70 is an ART record from 2023, so it is included in the 13 works in Q9.

Why: Rejects the premise and says W70 counts for Albury, whereas the key says it is credited to the Halloran Institute (last-listed affiliation), an affiliated institute that does not roll up to Albury

❌ R1 csv run 3 graded by llm

The premise is false. I04 sits under I03 and I02, so W70 is credited to the University of Albury through I04. I06 is also under I02, and W70 is included in the 13 works in Q9.

Why: Rejects the premise and credits W70 to the University of Albury, contradicting the key: only the last-listed affiliation counts (Halloran Institute), and as an affiliated institute rather than a unit it does not roll up to Albury.

❌ R1 csv run 4 graded by llm

The premise doesn't hold. W70 is credited to Albury through both I04 (Tessel, a unit of I03 and I02) and I06 (Halloran, a unit of I02), and it is an article, so it is one of Albury's research works (counted in Q9).

Why: Contradicts the checklist: it denies the premise and says W70 counts for Albury, whereas the key says only the last-listed affiliation counts and Halloran is an affiliated institute, not a unit.

❌ R1 csv run 5 graded by llm

The premise is false. W70 lists I04 (Tessel) and I06 (Halloran), and both sit under University of Albury (I04 → I03 → I02; I06 → I02). W70 is therefore one of Albury's 13 research works in Q9.

Why: Rejects the premise; the key says only the last-listed affiliation counts, so W70 goes to Halloran, an affiliated institute that doesn't roll up to Albury.

🟡 R10 executed run 1 graded by llm

W70's only credit is A08 at I06 (Halloran), not at I04 (Tessel). I06 is not in the Albury units (institution_units lists I06 only under itself), so W70 does not count. Even though I04 is an Albury unit, the premise doesn't hold because that credit is not on W70.

Q47 and Q48 have no field in the schema, so they are not in the JSON block.

Why: Says W70 is credited only to Halloran (I06) and that I06 is not an Albury unit, but never states the rule that only the last-listed affiliation counts, and doesn't call Halloran an affiliated institute.
