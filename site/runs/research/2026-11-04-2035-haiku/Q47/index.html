Why do Kesting University and Rostval Technical University swap places in 2024 between full and fractional counting?

Tests: Counting method: why the ranking flips.

Expected: Rostval is on four 2024 works (W21, W27, W28, W29), but on each it has one author of four: 4 x 1/4 = 1. Kesting is on three, with larger shares: W22 alone, half of W20, two of the four authors of W21: 2.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

Full counting credits each institution with every work it is on. Rostval is on four 2024 works (W21, W27, W28, W29), all with 4 authors, so it gets 4 full credits. Kesting gets 3 full credits from W20, W21 and W22, which give it 2.0 fractionally. Rostval's shares are only 1/4 per work (total 1.0), so the ranking swaps.

Why: Gets Rostval's four 4-author works at 1/4 each, but misses that Kesting's lead comes from a single-author review (W22) plus half shares of W20 and W21; the 2.0 total and the 'half' split are not explained, and the answer's account of full counting is muddled.

🟡 R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

Rostval has more works in full counting but less credit in fractional counting. **Reasoning:** Rostval's 4 works each have 4 authors and only one Rostval author, so it gets 0.25 per work (1.0 total). Kesting's works carry more of its own authors (W22 is single-author, W21 has 2 of 4) and total 2.0 despite fewer works.

Why: Gets Rostval's quarter-share per work right, but for Kesting it cites W22 and W21 (2 of 4) rather than W22 plus half of both W20 and W21, so the checklist's Kesting point is only partly met

🟡 R1 csv run 3 graded by llm

Full counting favours Rostval (4 works to Kesting's 3). Fractional counting favours Kesting (2.0 to Rostval's 1.0). Rostval's 2024 works each have four authors, only one of whom is from Rostval, so each earns only a quarter of a credit. Kesting's W22 is single-authored, and its other 2024 works carry larger shares.

Why: Gives the quarter-credit point for Rostval's four-author works, but only vaguely says Kesting has larger shares; omits that W22 is a single-author review and that Kesting holds half of W20 and W21.

🟡 R1 csv run 4 graded by llm

Rostval's 2024 works each have one Rostval author among four, so full counting gives it 4 works. Kesting's shared works give it fractional shares totalling 1.75–2.0, which beats Rostval's 1.0. Kesting leads on fractional counting, while Rostval leads on full counting.

Why: Gets Rostval's one-of-four authors and Kesting's larger fractional shares broadly, but doesn't name the single-author review W22 or the half-shares of W20 and W21, and it states a vague 1.75–2.0 range.

🟡 R1 csv run 5 graded by llm

Full counting gives Rostval 4 vs Kesting 3 because Rostval appears on more 2024 works. Fractional counting divides each work by its author count: Rostval's four 2024 works have one Rostval author each (1.0 total), while Kesting gets 1.0 from the single-authored W22 plus 0.5 each from W20 and W21 (2.0 total).

Why: Gets Kesting's shares right (W22 single-author, 0.5 each from W20 and W21) and Rostval's one author per work, but doesn't state four authors per work, so a quarter each (gives 1.0 total, which matches).
