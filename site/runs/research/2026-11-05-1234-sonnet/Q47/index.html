Why do Kesting University and Rostval Technical University swap places in 2024 between full and fractional counting?

Tests: Counting method: why the ranking flips.

Expected: Rostval is on four 2024 works (W21, W27, W28, W29), but on each it has one author of four: 4 x 1/4 = 1. Kesting is on three, with larger shares: W22 alone, half of W20, two of the four authors of W21: 2.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

Rostval has more works in 2024 (4 vs 3), but it is one of four authors on each, so each gives it 0.25. Kesting has a sole-authored W22 and half shares of W20 and W21, so it leads on fractional counting.

Why: Gives Kesting's W22 and half shares of W20/W21, but for Rostval says only 'one of four authors on each' giving 0.25, which is essentially the quarter-share point; however it never explicitly states that only one author per work is from Rostval, so it is nearly but not fully matched
