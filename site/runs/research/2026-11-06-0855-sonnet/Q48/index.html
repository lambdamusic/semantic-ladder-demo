Why is W70 not one of the University of Albury's research works, though its author lists the Tessel Laboratory?

Tests: Local rules: the last-listed affiliation, and affiliated institutes.

Expected: Tomasz Wierzba lists the Tessel Laboratory first and the Halloran Institute second; the house rule credits the last-listed one, Halloran. Halloran is an affiliated institute (AFF): its parent link is an agreement, so its works are not the University of Albury's.

🟡 R8 csv+axioms run 1 graded by llm

A08's credited affiliation on W70 is the one with the highest affiliation sequence, which is I06 (sequence 2). I06 is an affiliated institute and does not roll up to the University of Albury, so I04 does not count.

Why: Says the last-sequence affiliation counts and that the affiliated institute doesn't roll up to Albury, but does not name the Halloran Institute explicitly (only I06), and the Tessel Laboratory point is not addressed; mostly meets both points.

✅ R8 csv+axioms run 2 graded by llm

Credit goes to the author's highest-numbered affiliation on the work. On W70, A08 lists I04 (sequence 1) and I06 (sequence 2), so W70 is credited to I06, which is an affiliated institute that does not roll up to I02.

🟡 R8 csv+axioms run 3 graded by llm

A08 lists I04 first (sequence 1) and I06 second (sequence 2), and the highest sequence is the one credited. I06 is an affiliated institute, so it does not roll up into I02.

Why: Correctly says the last-listed (highest sequence) affiliation counts, so W70 goes to I06, and that I06 is an affiliated institute not rolling up to I02; but it doesn't state it as the Halloran Institute / not a unit explicitly in names, and uses IDs only. Substantively it meets both points, so this is a lenient partial.
