How many research works is the University of Albury (I02) credited with, its units included?

Tests: Hierarchy: the labs' works count, each work once; also versions, types, the affiliation rule and the affiliated institute.

Expected: count = 8

✅ R8 csv+axioms run 1 graded by code

8 (W01, W03, W06, W12, W13, W19, W71, W72). I06 is an affiliated institute and does not roll up to I02.

8: W01, W03, W06, W12, W13, W19, W71, W72. W70, W73 and W74 are credited only to I06. I06 is an affiliated institute, so it does not roll up to I02.
