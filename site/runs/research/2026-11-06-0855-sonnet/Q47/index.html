Why do Kesting University and Rostval Technical University swap places in 2024 between full and fractional counting?

Tests: Counting method: why the ranking flips.

Expected: Rostval is on four 2024 works (W21, W27, W28, W29), but on each it has one author of four: 4 x 1/4 = 1. Kesting is on three, with larger shares: W22 alone, half of W20, two of the four authors of W21: 2.

🟡 R8 csv+axioms run 1 graded by llm

I09 appears on more 2024 works, but as one of four authors each time, so it earns 0.25 per work. Kesting has fewer works but larger shares, including W22 where it is the sole author, so it leads under fractional counting.

Why: Gives the quarter-share point for Rostval (I09) and Kesting's larger shares including sole-author W22, but omits that Kesting holds half of W20 and W21.

🟡 R8 csv+axioms run 2 graded by llm

I09's 4 works in 2024 (W21, W27, W28, W29) each give it only one author share out of four, so it scores 1.0 under fractional counting. I07's three works (W20, W21, W22) have fewer co-authors, so it scores 2.0. I09 leads on full counting (4 against 3), and I07 leads on fractional counting.

Why: Gives the quarter-share point for Rostval (I09) but doesn't say W22 is single-author or that Kesting holds half of W20 and W21; it only says 'fewer co-authors'.

🟡 R8 csv+axioms run 3 graded by llm

Under full counting, I09 gets a whole work for each of its 4 papers, which is more than Kesting's 3. Under fractional counting, each of I09's papers has four authors and gives it only 0.25 (1.0 in total). Kesting's W22 is single-authored and counts fully, so it reaches 2.0.

Why: Gives Rostval's four-author quarter shares (1.0 total) and notes W22 single-author for Kesting, but omits Kesting's half shares of W20 and W21; the 2.0 total is only partly explained.

🟡 R8 csv+axioms run 4 graded by llm

Under full counting, I09 appears on four 2024 works to I07's three. Each of I09's four works is shared among four authors, so it earns only 0.25 each, 1 in total. I07 has the sole-authored W22 and the half-share W20, so it ends with 2.

Why: Correctly gives Rostval's quarter-shares, but the Kesting side omits W21's half-share, so its total of 2 doesn't follow from the works it cites (1 + 0.5 = 1.5).
