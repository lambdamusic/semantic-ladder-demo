Why do Kesting University and Rostval Technical University swap places in 2024 between full and fractional counting?

Tests: Counting method: why the ranking flips.

Expected: Rostval is on four 2024 works (W21, W27, W28, W29), but on each it has one author of four: 4 x 1/4 = 1. Kesting is on three, with larger shares: W22 alone, half of W20, two of the four authors of W21: 2.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

Rostval has more works in full counting (4 vs 3). In each of W21, W27, W28 and W29 it is only one of four authors, so it earns 0.25 per work (1.0 total). Kesting has W22 alone (1) plus half of W20 and W21, giving 2.0.

Why: Correctly gives Rostval's four works with one of four authors (0.25 each) and Kesting's W22 plus halves of W20 and W21, but it also says W21 is one of Rostval's works, which conflicts with the stated Kesting share, and it does not say Kesting's shares are larger.

🟡 R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

Fractional counting splits shared works. Kesting's W20 and W21 are each half shared with other institutions, giving 2.0 against Rostval's 4 works at a quarter-share each (1.0). Under full counting Rostval's 4 beats Kesting's 3.

Why: Correctly notes Rostval's works are quarter-shares and Kesting's W20/W21 are half-shares, but omits that each Rostval work has four authors with one from Rostval, and omits Kesting's single-author review W22; its 3-work full count is also not tied to W22.

🟡 R1 csv run 3 graded by llm

Rostval appears on more works (4 vs 3 in 2024), but on each it is one of four co-authors (0.25 share each). Kesting has the sole-author W22 (1) and half-shares of W20 and W21, giving 2.0 vs 1.0.

Why: Gives Rostval's quarter share per work and Kesting's W22 plus half-shares of W20/W21, but says Rostval has 4 works in 2024 vs Kesting 3 only partly matching; the answer states Rostval 4 works, 0.25 each, so it meets both points, though it never explicitly says 'four authors with one from Rostval' as a fixed fact for all four works

✅ R1 csv run 4 graded by llm

Rostval's four 2024 works each have four authors, so it gets 1/4 of each (1.0 in total). Kesting has the single-author W22 and half of W20 and W21 (2.0 in total).

🟡 R1 csv run 5 graded by llm

Full counting gives one credit per work, so Rostval has 4 against Kesting's 3. Fractional counting weights by author share: Rostval's four 2024 works are all multi-author and give 0.25 each (1.0 total). Kesting has the single-author W22 (1.0) plus half shares of W20 and W21 (2.0 total).

Why: Covers Rostval's quarter shares (0.25 each) and Kesting's W22 plus half of W20/W21, but doesn't state that each work has four authors with one from Rostval; it also doesn't explicitly say the ranking swaps, though that is implied.

Why: Gets Rostval's quarter shares (four authors each) and Kesting's larger shares (W22 single author, half of W20/W21), but says 4 works for Rostval vs the key's four works too; matches mostly, yet uses IDs I09/I07 without naming the institutions and omits that W22 is a review

Why: Gets Rostval's quarter shares (1.0 total) and Kesting's single-author W22, but omits Kesting's half shares of W20 and W21 and doesn't state the swap direction explicitly
