## The data

You can't see the data, but you can query it. It is in an RDF store, described below. Write SPARQL 1.1 SELECT or ASK queries.

To run queries, reply with one or more fenced blocks (```sparql), and nothing else is needed:
each is run and its results sent back to you. You have up to 8 rounds of queries.
When you have what you need, reply with the final answers and no queries.

Classes and the properties their resources use:

- `res:Author`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:name`, `res:orcid`, `res:samePersonAs`
- `res:Authorship`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:affiliationSeq`, `res:author`, `res:authorPosition`, `res:institution`, `res:work`
- `res:Citation`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:citedWork`, `res:citingWork`
- `res:CreditedAuthorship`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:affiliationSeq`, `res:author`, `res:authorPosition`, `res:institution`, `res:work`
- `res:Institution`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:country`, `res:countsTowards`, `res:institutionType`, `res:name`, `res:parent`, `res:ror`, `res:ultimateParent`, `res:unitOf`
- `res:ResearchWork`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:cites`, `res:countsAs`, `res:creditedCountry`, `res:creditedInstitution`, `res:doi`, `res:hasPerson`, `res:recordSource`, `res:retracted`, `res:source`, `res:title`, `res:workType`, `res:year`
- `res:Source`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:issn`, `res:name`, `res:sourceType`, `res:tier`
- `res:Work`: `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `res:cites`, `res:countsAs`, `res:creditedCountry`, `res:creditedInstitution`, `res:doi`, `res:hasPerson`, `res:recordSource`, `res:retracted`, `res:source`, `res:title`, `res:versionOf`, `res:workType`, `res:year`

The store holds the data after the rules of its axioms have run, with no definitions alongside. Besides the data's own properties, it holds what the rules derived: res:samePersonAs links the author records of one person; res:countsAs gives the work a record counts as (a preprint's published version, or itself), and the class res:ResearchWork holds the works that count as research output; res:hasPerson gives each work's distinct people, one author record each; res:creditedInstitution and res:creditedCountry give the institutions and countries credited on a work, every level up; res:unitOf, res:countsTowards and res:ultimateParent give the hierarchy; res:cites links the works of the citations that count; res:tierRank ranks the journal tiers, 1 the highest.
