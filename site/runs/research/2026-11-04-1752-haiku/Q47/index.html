Why do Kesting University and Rostval Technical University swap places in 2024 between full and fractional counting?

Tests: Counting method: why the ranking flips.

Expected: Rostval is on four 2024 works (W21, W27, W28, W29), but on each it has one author of four: 4 x 1/4 = 1. Kesting is on three, with larger shares: W22 alone, half of W20, two of the four authors of W21: 2.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

Rostval has more full works (4 vs 3) but Kesting has more fractional credit (2.0 vs 1.0). Rostval's 2024 works each have four authors, only one of them from Rostval, so each contributes 0.25. Kesting's 2024 works have fewer authors per work (W22 has one, W20 has two), so its shares are larger. The data has no separate field for this question, so the explanation is derived from the author counts.

Why: Gives the quarter-share for Rostval and the larger Kesting shares via W22 and W20, but omits W21 and the half-share of W20 and W21 detail; mostly matches both points though less specific
