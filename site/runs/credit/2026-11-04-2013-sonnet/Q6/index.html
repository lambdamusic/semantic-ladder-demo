Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

✅ R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The data doesn't state a reason. It shows a Valdoria Country Ceiling of BB (A12), a foreign-currency rating of BB (A14) and a local-currency rating of BBB- (A13), which is consistent with the ceiling capping the foreign-currency rating.

✅ R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

Valdoria's Country Ceiling is BB (A12). That caps the foreign-currency rating at BB (A14), while the local-currency rating is BBB- (A13). The ceiling is the likely cause, though the data does not state a reason outright.

✅ R1 csv run 3 graded by llm

The foreign-currency rating is capped by the sovereign Country Ceiling of BB (A12). The local-currency rating of BBB- (A13) is not constrained by it, so FC is BB (A14).
