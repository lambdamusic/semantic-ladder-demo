Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

✅ R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The data does not state a reason. E05's foreign-currency rating (BB, A14) equals the Republic of Valdoria's country ceiling (BB, A12), which is consistent with the ceiling capping FC ratings, while its local-currency rating is BBB- (A13).

The foreign-currency rating is capped by Valdoria's country ceiling of BB (A12, rating type CC), while the local-currency rating BBB- (A13) is not subject to that cap. The data doesn't state the mechanism explicitly, so this rests on the meaning of CC.
