- `instruments` (8 rows): instrument_id, description, issuer_id, guarantor_id, credit_risk_of, seniority, currency, local_currency, maturity

An empty valid_to means the rating was still in force on 1 October 2026. ancestors lists every entity above each entity in its chain of parents, with how many levels up.
