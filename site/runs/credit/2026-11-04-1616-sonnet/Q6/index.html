Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The data points to the sovereign country ceiling. VD's country ceiling is BB (A12), and E05's foreign-currency rating is BB (A14) while its local-currency rating is BBB- (A13). The data does not state the reason explicitly.

Why: Names the BB country ceiling as the likely cause, but never says the ceiling caps foreign-currency ratings and not local-currency ones, and hedges that the data doesn't state the reason.

🟡 R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

The data doesn't state a reason. It does show that VD's country ceiling is BB (A12), and E05's FC rating is BB (A14), so the cap is the likely cause. E05's LC rating is BBB- (A13).

Why: Names the BB country ceiling as the likely cause, but never states that the ceiling caps foreign-currency ratings and not local-currency ones; it hedges that the data gives no reason.
