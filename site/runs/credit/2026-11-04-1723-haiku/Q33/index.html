Whose credit risk does a holder of the Halden Leasing 2028 notes take, and how is it rated?

Tests: Roles: the guarantor is the parent, not the ultimate parent.

Expected: risk_of = E10 rating = BBB+

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 1 graded by code

E10 (Halden Bank), rated BBB+. I05 is guaranteed by E10, so its holders take E10's credit risk, and A29 rates I05 at BBB+, matching E10's A27 rating.

JSON: {"risk_of": "E10", "rating": "BBB+"}

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 2 graded by code

E10, Halden Bank AG, at BBB+. I05 is guaranteed by E10 (A28), so its holders carry E10's risk, and E10's rating is BBB+ (A27).

JSON: {"risk_of": "E10", "rating": "BBB+"}

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 3 graded by code

Holders take E10's credit risk, rated BBB+. I05 is guaranteed by E10 (not its issuer E11), and A29 (2026-04-15) rates it BBB+, which matches E10's current LTIDR under A27.

JSON: {"risk_of": "E10", "rating": "BBB+"}
