Whose credit risk does a holder of the Northwind Finance 2030 notes take, and how is it rated?

Tests: Roles: issuer versus guarantor; a finance subsidiary's guaranteed debt carries the guarantor's risk.

Expected: risk_of = E02 rating = A

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 1 graded by code

Northwind Holdings (E02), rated A. I03 is guaranteed by E02, so holders take E02's risk, and E02's rating is A (A08; A09 affirmed it).

JSON: {"risk_of": "E02", "rating": "A"}

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 2 graded by code

Northwind Holdings (E02). I03 is guaranteed by E02, so its holders carry E02's credit risk, and it is rated A (A11).

JSON: {"risk_of": "E02", "rating": "A"}

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 3 graded by code

Holders take E02's credit risk, rated A. I03 is guaranteed by E02 (not its issuer E04), and A11 (2025-09-15) rates it A, matching E02's current rating under A09.

JSON: {"risk_of": "E02", "rating": "A"}
