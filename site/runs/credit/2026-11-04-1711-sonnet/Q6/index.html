Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The data doesn't say. A13 (LC BBB-) and A14 (FC BB) have no comment, and A12 only shows a Valdoria country ceiling of BB.

Why: Mentions the Valdoria country ceiling of BB but doesn't name it as the cause or say it caps only foreign-currency ratings; says the data doesn't explain it.

The data gives no explanation. It only shows that the FC rating of BB (A14) equals Valdoria's country ceiling of BB (A12), while the LC rating is BBB- (A13).

Why: Notes FC rating BB equals the country ceiling BB, but says no explanation is given and never states the ceiling caps FC and not LC ratings as the cause.
