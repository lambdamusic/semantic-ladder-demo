Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The Country Ceiling for Valdoria is BB (A12), which caps foreign-currency ratings. Valdoria Telecom's local-currency rating is BBB- (A13) and its foreign-currency rating is BB (A14).

Why: Names the BB Country Ceiling as capping foreign-currency ratings, but does not say it doesn't cap local-currency ratings (only implied by the BBB- local rating).

✅ R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

Valdoria's Country Ceiling is BB (A12), which caps the foreign-currency rating at BB (A14). The local-currency rating of BBB- (A13) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 3 graded by llm

The data doesn't state a reason. The FC rating of BB (A14) equals the Valdoria country ceiling of BB (A12), while the LC rating is BBB- (A13), which suggests the ceiling is capping the FC rating.

Why: Identifies the BB country ceiling as likely capping the FC rating, but hedges that the data states no reason and does not explicitly say the ceiling applies to FC and not LC ratings.
