Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The data records the gap (local currency BBB- in A13, foreign currency BB in A14, both 2025-04-01) but gives no reason. The sovereign ceiling of BB (A12) would explain it, but the data does not say so.

Why: Mentions the BB sovereign ceiling as a possible explanation but hedges and doesn't assert it as the cause, and never says the ceiling caps foreign-currency but not local-currency ratings.

🟡 R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

The data does not give a reason. The FC rating (BB, A14) is below the LC rating (BBB-, A13), and BB equals Valdoria's country ceiling (VD, CC, BB, A12), which would be consistent with a ceiling cap but is not stated.

Why: Identifies that FC rating (BB) equals the country ceiling (BB) but hedges that the cause is not stated, and does not say the ceiling caps FC and not LC ratings.
