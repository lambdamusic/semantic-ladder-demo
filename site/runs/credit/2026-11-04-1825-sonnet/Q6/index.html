Why is Valdoria Telecom's foreign-currency rating lower than its local-currency rating?

Tests: Ceilings: a Country Ceiling is a cap on foreign-currency ratings, not a rating of the sovereign or of the company.

Expected: Because of the Country Ceiling for Valdoria (BB), which caps the foreign-currency ratings of entities domiciled there (transfer and convertibility risk). The local-currency rating (BBB-) is not capped.

🟡 R1 csv run 1 graded by llm

The data does not say. It shows only the different ratings, LC BBB- (A13) and FC BB (A14), with no comment. The gap is consistent with the Valdoria country ceiling of BB (A12), but that is inference.

Why: Mentions the BB country ceiling as consistent with the gap but hedges it as inference, and does not state that the ceiling caps foreign-currency but not local-currency ratings.

✅ R1 csv run 2 graded by llm

Valdoria's Country Ceiling is BB (A12), which caps the foreign-currency rating at BB (A14). The local-currency rating is BBB- (A13).

🟡 R1 csv run 3 graded by llm

The data doesn't state the cause. The foreign-currency rating is BB (A14), equal to Valdoria's Country Ceiling of BB (A12). The local-currency rating is BBB- (A13), so the ceiling plausibly caps the foreign-currency rating.

Why: Identifies the BB Country Ceiling and that it plausibly caps the foreign-currency rating, but hedges that the cause isn't stated and doesn't clearly say local-currency ratings are not capped.

✅ R1 csv run 4 graded by llm

The data points to the country ceiling. Valdoria's ceiling is BB (A12), the foreign-currency rating is capped at BB (A14), and the local-currency rating is BBB- (A13). The data states no reason beyond this.

🟡 R1 csv run 5 graded by llm

The data shows no comment explaining it. It does show a Valdoria country ceiling of BB (A12) and a foreign-currency rating of BB for E05 (A14) against a local-currency rating of BBB- (A13). The foreign-currency rating appears to be held at the ceiling.

Why: Names the BB country ceiling and says FC rating is held at it, but never states that the ceiling caps foreign-currency and not local-currency ratings, and hedges on causation ('appears').

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 1 graded by llm

Valdoria's Country Ceiling is BB (A12), which caps the FC rating (A14, BB). The LC rating (A13, BBB-) is not capped.

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 2 graded by llm

The data supports this reading: the Country Ceiling for VD is BB (A12), and it caps foreign-currency ratings but not local-currency ones. E05's FC rating is BB (A14) and its LC rating is BBB- (A13).

✅ R5 csv+ontology run 3 graded by llm
