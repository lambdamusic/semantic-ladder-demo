Each table puts a dataset's latest finished runs side by side: a column per run, a row per condition, with accuracy and its 95% interval. Only runs on the dataset's current questions are shown. Accuracy is the mean over questions, correct = 1 and partial = ½, of each question's score across the repeats, and the interval is a bootstrap over questions. Click a column's heading for the run.

What the numbers mean for each goal is in the [findings](/findings/). Every run, including the older ones, is under [all runs](/runs/).
