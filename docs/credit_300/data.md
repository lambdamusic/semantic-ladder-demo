The base data plus 300 invented issuers, written by `scale.py` from a fixed seed. The added issuers repeat the base data's traps: some have a RESEARCH record with a different country or holding the latest action, some are QA test records with actions of their own, and there are group chains, finance subsidiaries with guaranteed notes and credit opinions, instruments in local currency, expected ratings, withdrawals, Watches, outlook changes, and rows out of date order.
